MAKE SOMETHING USEFUL

Your design. Their daily life.

  1. Tap a place to inspect it. Tap open ground to walk.
  2. Open Build, choose a tool and place it. A green preview marks a possible position.
  3. Run a market day. Follow the real delivery cart and inspect stock, losses and power.
  4. Pause, change your design, then try again. The clock waits while you plan.
  5. Reach twelve households with limited losses, a resident delivery team and a maintenance reserve. Then test your design in rain.

Drag to rotate; pinch or +/− to zoom. Use the walking arrows or WASD to move. Q/E turn the view and R/F tilt. Drag with two fingers to pan. The centre button returns to your character.

Unused structures can be reclaimed for their fictional purchase cost. Their effects stop immediately. A failed delivery trial can restart without changing your builds; trial earnings only become reserve after a successful day.
