      PIPUPATH · THE UNBUILT CITY

      How to play and build your city

      You are the builder. Solve problems that matter to people, test your ideas and watch your choices change one connected city.

      Tap a completed building, or choose City menu → Inspect buildings, to test and operate its district service. The workshop includes Guided, Standard and Expert contracts plus larger text, high contrast and battery-saving settings.

      Start your first city problem to unlock its building challenge.

      1. Understand the problem. Meet a person or neighbourhood with a need. Read the situation before making your choice.
      2. Make your decision. Choose what you would do and see its consequences in the city. Some problems test priorities; others also need construction.
      3. Build when your challenge asks you to. Choose Straight, Bend or Junction and tap an empty cell to place a connector. Then choose Rotate and tap a piece already on the grid to turn that piece 90 degrees clockwise per tap. Keep tapping the same piece until its openings face the right way. Rotate stays selected until you tap it again or choose another tool. Go around trees and blocked spaces and stay within budget.
      4. Test, improve and try again. Press Test my network. If a service does not connect, adjust the pieces and test again. Use Need a hint if you're stuck. You cannot skip a required build to reach the next question.
      5. See what you built. Press See what I built in my city. Watch the structure appear and see which fictional services work better. There is one short celebration, then Continue.
      6. Keep the city working. Open City menu → City services to inspect buildings and test a broken link. Tap an orange-marked connection, test the repair and restore the service. The same city keeps evolving.
      7. Take one idea into real life. Tap Start my real Quest above the game. PipuPath suggests an editable, safe action based on your city service. You choose what to attempt, save real evidence and reflect honestly. Playing the city alone does not earn verified XP.
      Explore the city and use the controls

      Tap a destination to let your character walk there. Drag to look around, pinch or scroll to zoom, and use the City map for an overview. On a phone, use the on-screen movement controls when available; on a keyboard use WASD or the arrow keys.

      The City menu shows Decisions, My City, the Build Lab, City services, the Builder Shop and other chapters. Your virtual Builder Credits can purchase multiple flower gardens, trees, lights and other decorations across built districts. Character accessories are unlocked once and can be equipped or removed. You can revisit completed decisions and working builds; unfinished future challenges remain locked until you complete the current problem.

      Pause, save, sound and music

      Every completed challenge waits for Continue. Use Pause and Resume to return to the required next step. Saving is optional and stays on this device only; it does not automatically follow you to another phone.

      Tap 🔊/🔇 for sound effects or ♫ for background music. The speaker previews a sound. They have separate controls. Open City menu → Music and sound volume to adjust each level. Music stops when you leave the game screen. You can switch either off whenever you like.

      Chapters 5–12 and returning to my city

      Finish Chapter 4 to unlock sixteen new challenges across Chapters 5–12. Investigate evidence, sequence safe repairs, plan homes and transport, respond to emergencies, protect food, design inclusive spaces, sort waste, balance energy, negotiate public decisions, assign specialists, forecast growth and build a resilient city. Each activity has its own controls. Try your plan, tap Test my solution and revise if needed before applying it. The large Continue my city button resumes your current challenge. Previously completed advanced challenges remain recorded. Chapter 12 ends with your city showcase.

      Finish the linked city decisions and builds to unlock additional replayable neighbourhood challenges in City services. Each challenge still starts with a decision and is completed by testing a real repair or network solution.

      The city's demand numbers, materials and effects are fictional learning simulations, not real engineering instructions, real money or verified XP. The goal is to learn to notice problems, think carefully and build useful solutions in real life.
