MAKE YOURSELF AT HOME

Your world. Your moves.

Drag the scene to orbit. Scroll or pinch to zoom. Tap once on the ground and your character walks there automatically, routing around walls and equipment. Tap a station label to walk over and interact on arrival. Choose a new destination to change course, or use Stop. WASD / arrow keys also respect obstacles. On a phone, use the arrow controls.

Follow the yellow Take me there button to meet the city builders one at a time. You can explore freely whenever you want. Use City map to see the whole neighbourhood, then Follow me to return to your character. Later, you can build, test and improve the whole city.

All energy units and costs are fictional. This is a learning simulation, not electrical installation guidance. Sound is optional. Progress lasts only while this game stays open.
