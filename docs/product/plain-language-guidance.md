# Plain-language guidance — 15 October 2026

Owner-authorised audience clarification: PipuPath serves the general public,
including adults of all ages, as well as younger users. This supersedes student-
only assumptions in older experience guidance. It does not relax minor safety,
consent, privacy, evidence or progression rules.

## Shared voice

`src/lib/ai/builder-language.ts` supplies the same language instructions to the
Profile, Mission, Journey, Quest, Economic Pathways and Builder Guide providers,
including retained Gemini adapters. Speak to one person with short, concrete,
respectful instructions. Do not assume school, employment, business ownership,
paid tools or money. Use supplied context rather than decorating generic advice
with a name. Avoid jargon, exaggerated praise, forced slang and invented facts.
No extra AI review call is introduced.

Each Quest should say what to do first, what to save and when it is done.
Generated reflection questions follow tried/result/learned/change order. Legacy
saved prompts are available together in an optional disclosure rather than
attached to unrelated fixed reflection fields.

## Non-AI quests

The fallback still uses only approved milestone context and remains deterministic.
It now distinguishes finding out, practising and making a first attempt. It names
the milestone and expected outcome, then asks for a real attempt, a check and use
of what was learned. Skill labels are no longer listed as physical resources.
Minors retain supervision guidance; adults are not automatically sent to teachers.

These are three contextual templates, not equivalent to a bespoke AI-generated
activity. They do not see previous private reflections, infer missing resources,
or certify that a broad milestone outcome has been achieved. Existing completion
and XP rules remain unchanged. Wider fallback specialisation and history-aware
context need a separate evidence-led improvement; do not bypass the minor
external-AI restriction to obtain personalisation.

## Release and evaluation

Prompt versions are advanced for newly generated records. Existing saved Missions,
Journeys, Quests and completed evidence are not rewritten. Builder Guide cached
results may remain reusable for the existing six-hour window. No schema migration,
external-AI permission expansion or automatic regeneration is introduced.

Technical owner: product/release operator. Before release, compare synthetic
samples for a younger learner, an adult developing a service, an adult practising
a creative skill, an older adult and an unspecified setting. Check whether the
person can explain the first action, required proof and finish point without a
facilitator translating. Check no school assumption, invented access, income
promise or unsafe contact. Include failed-attempt and no-internet cases.

Automated tests verify fallback branching, adult/minor separation, schema bounds,
prompt wiring and reflection form submission. They do not establish generated
model quality or user comprehension. Those require real generation samples and
user observation. Measure first-action understanding, completion and return to
useful action rather than screen time. Update this shared standard from findings
so the founder does not have to rewrite individual quests.
