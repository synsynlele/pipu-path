# The Unbuilt City — Chapter 2: all fourteen places

Date: 28 October 2026 · Development preview; not production.

## Chapter progression

**Chapter 1: The Foundation.** Preserve the existing 16 problems in
Repair (4), Power (4), Food (4) and Learning (4). Every selected answer
remains a physical, reversible construction state. No player can open
Chapter 2 questions without completing these foundation decisions.

**Chapter 2: The Growing City.** All ten previously undeveloped places
now receive two authored problems each: Housing, Water, Healthcare,
Transport, Technology, Business, Environment, Governance, Creative
Economy and Industry. Twenty new choices make 36 total. Each problem
has three choices with benefits and tradeoffs, one independent site,
immediate 3D feedback, and reversible persistence. The ten districts
retain their existing distinct architecture rather than rebuilding the
city or adding disconnected mini-games.

When the 16th foundation answer finishes, the camera reveals the
individual city and highlights next-stage development. The player can
tap Start now or continue automatically. The Chapter 2 reveal may
be paused, resumed, and restored from an existing opted-in device
save. Existing saves completed before this release open the reveal.

## Real consequences (fictional model)

The city model now includes fourteen districts rather than only four.
New dependency links include Power → Water, Water → Healthcare,
Environment → Housing/Transport, Transport → Food/Healthcare, and
Industry → Repair. Chapter 2 scenes state a consequence of a
specific earlier district in the saved city—struggling, active or
thriving—so not every learner sees precisely the same setup.
Buildings show three distinct physical forms per option. Across
36 problems this creates 108 option-specific architectural states;
one selected state per problem is displayed at a time.

These are simplified author-created exercises for learning tradeoffs,
not professional advice about construction, medicine, sanitation,
transport, manufacturing or regulated financial activities. No real
construction methods, health decisions, company investments, grades,
XP, personal assessment or identity are generated.

## Student control contract

Retain the original horizontal mobile nav, Pause/Resume, Decisions,
Last decision, My City, City map, Build and Explore. The casebook has
14 sections, 36 choices, visible Chapter 2 locks before completion,
and clear revision of previously answered problems. Guided travel
moves directly to the next problem; long trips use an abbreviated
camera transition instead of forcing long-distance avatar walking.
Every action remains recoverable from an in-session checkpoint and,
after explicit opt-in, from the same-browser sanitized snapshot.

## Still to build

Chapter 3: operational entrepreneurship, real production/trade
flows, business/customer simulation and jobs. Chapter 4: ongoing,
reactive population, resource and resilience simulation, civic
leadership, safe unexpected events, improvements and stewardship.
Neither is represented as already available. Additional calibration
and broader student testing will be needed before any credible
quantitative claims about city service conditions.

## Verification

- 14 distinct districts; 4×4 original cases plus 10×2 new cases
- 3 distinct options per scenario, persistent replacements and
  immediate city effects; 108 possible shapes
- all 16 foundation choices needed to unlock Chapter 2
- chapter reveal and recoverJourney("chapter") survive interruption
- selected answers balanced over three positions for all 36 cases
- option changes recalculate neighbour dependencies
- original opted-in saves continue to restore; no new private data
- Android portrait/tablet touch navigation, chapter reveal,
  performance, frame rate and fallback display require human tests
