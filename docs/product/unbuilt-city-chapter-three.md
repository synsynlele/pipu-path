# The Unbuilt City — Chapter 3: City of Opportunities

**Status:** playable first operating simulation in development; not production.
**Date:** 28 October 2026.

## Entry

Complete the existing 16 foundation plus 20 growing-city decisions. The
last outcome now offers **Start Chapter 3 — Opportunities**. After this,
the navigation also includes a permanent **Opportunities** control.
A returning learner who finished 36 decisions can open it directly.
Before the required decisions, the new control is visibly locked.

## Player's seven-day challenge

Each simulated day starts with a different simple civic/economic pressure:
normal demand, busy delivery day, hotter weather, equipment failure,
apprentice arrival, market day and final city-wide check. The player
chooses one operating priority before clicking **Run city day**:

- **Balance services:** divide water between clinics and food production.
- **People first:** supply clinic needs before using limited water at farms.
- **Grow enterprise:** support food production and retail demand first,
  acknowledging possible healthcare access trade-offs.
- **Repair first:** allocate more parts to repair teams, improving
  tomorrow's capacity if repairs are completed.

The operating chain is actually calculated from the completed city:
waterworks produce limited water, industry makes spare parts, maintenance
teams receive parts, farms convert allocated water into food,
shops receive and serve food, clinics receive water. Later repair
activity can modestly improve the next day's production. Goods and
stocks are bounded; the model cannot produce infinite supplies.

The resulting screen shows produced units, deliveries, clinic and
market visits, repairs, work activities, unserved needs and carried
inventory. The city scene displays routes for **actual last-day
shipments** after Chapter 3 starts, never pretending to show a
delivery when none occurred. The seven-day history makes the
consequences of each operating decision visible.

## Education and safety

This is authored fictional practice in resource allocation, enterprise
operations, cause and effect and ethical trade-offs. Do not market
these simplified simulated numbers as real health statistics, jobs,
profit, verified skills or achievements. It does not assign XP or
affect existing PipuPath accounts. All choices stay reversible.
A player can restart the seven-day practice without losing their
built city; earlier decisions alter subsequent runs rather than
silently rewriting history.

The change adds only a **small, scrollable panel** rather than
another full-screen card. City View, 3D navigation, manual pauses,
Chapter 1/2 flow, answer revision and the pre-existing two-day
legacy energy exercise remain separate and usable. The same
consented on-device save format remains backward-compatible and
only stores validated strategy IDs and bounded numerical totals.

- Real shipments become visible on the existing 3D road network.

## Founder-operated enterprise extension (development branch)

Players now establish one of four starter businesses: Clean Water Service,
Food Growers, Repair Team or Neighbourhood Shop. This is an operating role
inside one connected city, not a disconnected minigame. Players can revise
their choice before future days and choose to operate steadily, improve
tools using one finite spare part or invite more customers (which can
increase unmet demand when supplies are scarce).

The selected business records its own activity, service visits, workers
active and unmet demand after each day. A reusable stall and small number
of animated people appear in the selected real district, driven by the
latest recorded operating results. From the Chapter 3 panel, the player can
open the existing aerial view focused on their enterprise. Earlier chapter
decisions still determine the city's service capacities.

This first release is **not** an independent detailed finance simulator:
there are no fictional profit claims presented as real income, verified
employment results or awards. Enterprise operations share the city's
finite resources, so improvement is a trade-off. Previously saved
seven-day records without ownership still load; new records are allowlisted
and bounded under the existing consented device-only save.

**Next milestone:** customer orders, apprenticeships, skill transfer and
meaningful collaboration between enterprises, validated first with students.
Chapter 4 will introduce time-based resident needs, maintenance and city resilience only after Chapter 3 is validated in real classroom use.
