# Unbuilt City — seamless decision-flow design

Date: 28 October 2026. Preview only.

## Learner problem
Students reported unnecessary manual taps after each decision and noticed
that the best-authored response was always the first choice.

## Updated experience
1. Open Problems (16), select a scenario once.
2. Avatar travels to the location automatically.
3. See a scenario and select one of the three choices; this is the only
   compulsory tap for each subsequent problem.
4. The selected fictional decision is kept immediately, not behind another
   confirmation. Construction animates at the district location.
5. The consequence appears in a concise panel for 7.2 seconds, including
   what changed and the trade-off. Connected sectors and safe observation
   are available on demand.
6. The game automatically travels to the next scenario, including the
   next district after four cases. No extra navigation buttons required.
7. After all sixteen scenarios, show a completion panel. No forced loop,
   monetisation, grade, verified XP or real-world competence claim.

Optional controls remain for accessibility and individual preference:
Pause to read, Replay action, Change decision and Next now. Screen-off
pauses auto-advance rather than silently skipping learning content.
Manual navigation and menus cancel any pending automatic transition.

## Answer positions
The first-authored choice was often the most responsible one. To prevent
position-based guessing, the choice order is rotated using the case index
and a random, session-only integer from 0–2. The other two options are
permuted using a case-key hash. For each session, the authored first
choice appears five or six times in each of the three positions over the
sixteen cases. The order stays stable through retries within that session.
Case semantics and choice IDs do not change.

## What this is not
Not an AI assessment, not learning efficacy evidence, and not
independent engineering/food-safety guidance. This UI orchestration does
not extend the two-day connected economy or activate the remaining ten
future district models. Device save is optional, local and excludes
personal reflection and real Quest evidence.

## Test gates
- All sixteen cases offer unique, stable answer orders with balanced
  positions under varied session seeds.
- Selecting any choice instantly records the fictional decision, and
  the city action starts without confirmation.
- Outcome automatically advances without extra taps; the last case
  ends gracefully, with no looping.
- Pausing, retrying, replaying, navigating elsewhere or closing the
  panel leaves no stray timer that hijacks the user.
- Android portrait visibility and read-time comfort, route completion,
  reduced-motion handling, offline and device save behaviour.
- Student observation: self-directed choices, attention to effects,
  comprehension and voluntary continuation.
