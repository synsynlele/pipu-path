# Character-driven market street pilot

The rejected drainage board is retired. The preview now enters the existing Harvest Exchange district with the existing player character and city scenery.

## Play

Tap timber or roof sheets to walk over and collect them. Tap either damaged stall to carry the material there and repair its frame or fit a roof. Items remain visible on the character while being carried; the character works only after arriving. Both stalls must provide shelter.

Tap the abandoned cart to push it, then choose where to park it. Parking in the storage doorway clears the entrance but obstructs the store. Players can recover by moving the cart to the clear bay. Carry the produce crate into the dry store. Ring the market bell to test the work at any time: unmet needs produce specific feedback; a complete working site opens with traders, produce and customers moving through the repaired market.

Tap open ground to walk freely, drag horizontally to turn the camera, or use the object buttons in Find an object for keyboard access. Stop walking cancels the pending action. Return item releases an ordinary carried item back to its collection point; the cart must be parked. Restart resets this fictional worksite. Exit returns to the entrance and restores the campaign character position and original market scenery.

## Boundaries

The pilot is session-only and does not replace or advance the authored campaign, grant Builder Credits, or create real-world evidence. Reusable actions (collect, carry, repair, push, store, test) and deterministic rules are separate from scene rendering. Multiple repair orders work. No action executes remotely when a route is blocked. This is a repair/interaction pilot, not a complete city construction platform or a physical engineering simulation.

## Verification

Model tests cover dependency order, carrying limits, early tests, doorway obstruction and recovery. The actual game controller walks the original avatar through the whole mission, opens the market and restores campaign state on exit. Browser and phone-accessible preview verification follow implementation; physical-phone comfort and enjoyment still require player feedback.

## City construction extension

The Worksite selector now offers six additional, real-city construction sites: skyline tower, homes, hospital, community farm, stadium and regional airport. The character collects a site kit, carries it to the base, operates the structure control, connects services and tests the site before opening. Actions require arrival; early opening tests report missing work. Farm irrigation visibly grows crop rows; tower work uses a crane; the other sites use their own named construction controls and distinct geometry.

Finished models use curated recipes, aligned entrances and paved/landscaped edges. The airport is a small fictional representation, not a full-scale airport simulation. All six sites reserve existing road clearance. Finished project geometry is merged by worksite and cached by phase to limit draw calls. The market also gains aligned paving and planters after a successful opening. Cosmetic finishing never opens an incomplete site.

Swept personal-space checks cover the player, original residents, visible background pedestrians, traders and customers. Moving customers steer or wait; the player can replan around them. Spawn slots are checked before creating customers. The pilot remains session-only and is separate from the twelve-chapter campaign and its backups. This is the first construction catalogue, not a claim that every authored chapter has been converted.

## Meaningful-builder episode and crews

The preview starts with investigation, care operations and teaching rather than construction. Every action requires the original avatar to reach its object. Inspecting an already working pump consumes a spare without fixing the dry tap; inspecting the supply and operating its closed valve restores flow. Clinic arrivals expose the need for both reception and support staff plus stock. Apprentice success requires accessible tools and enough teaching support; the player must stand away during the independent trial. Outcomes remain explicitly fictional and session-only.

A completed prototype seeds a bounded crew batch on unique, reserved city parcels. Caps and suitable districts prevent unbounded towers or duplicated airports. Replaying re-derives the same layout rather than adding more copies. The episode extends relevant batches; the full twelve-chapter fill plan is still a design, not a completed conversion.

## Bottom guidance, worksite framing and the existing shop

Mission guidance sits directly over the bottom of the city, with a transparent background. The production toolbar stays at the top, including the original Builder Shop button and live Builder Credit balance. The worksite camera fits the character and the useful work area in portrait and wide screens, reserving room below. Build actions retain a bounded closer framing; obstructions cause an orbit or higher view rather than a camera pressed against a building.

Tested chapter completion awards 30 fictional Builder Credits through the existing shop ledger, once per chapter across both game modes. Replaying or restarting cannot farm credits. The hands-on celebration displays new credits; the production reward popup does not stack on it. Completed water, market, clinic and learning outcomes unlock the matching decoration districts. Existing items, prices, character cosmetics and purchase rendering are reused. Shopping pauses physical work. Hands-on progress remains session-only; no new save support is claimed.

The current scope remains 12 playable primary challenges. The other 24 distinct missions needed for the 36-challenge journey are unimplemented. Reusing the production catalogue is the content source for that expansion, not a completion claim or a dated delivery commitment.
