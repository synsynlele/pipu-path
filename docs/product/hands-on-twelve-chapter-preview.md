# Twelve-chapter hands-on preview

This release supplies one primary useful outcome in each chapter. It does not implement all thirty-six missions in the broader blueprint. Existing saved campaigns and real-world Quests are separate. Progress in this experimental journey lasts for the current page session.

| Chapter                        | Field                           | Physical work and tested outcome                                                                                                          |
| ------------------------------ | ------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1. Notice what matters         | Observation, public service     | Follow the water supply, establish the cause and restore tested flow.                                                                     |
| 2. Make things work again      | Repair, useful spaces           | Restore the market's shelter, entrances and protected stock.                                                                              |
| 3. Grow and share enough       | Agriculture, food security      | Adjust soil, irrigation and heat protection; produce at least four healthy crates without wasted water.                                   |
| 4. Care for people             | Health, service operations      | Staff reception and support, stock supplies and serve four arrivals. No diagnosis or medical treatment is simulated.                      |
| 5. Help others learn           | Education, human development    | Prepare tools, demonstrate and improve support until the apprentice succeeds independently.                                               |
| 6. Invent useful tools         | Engineering, technology         | Build a stable support arrangement and test a cart carrying all three crates.                                                             |
| 7. Bring people together       | Arts, sport, inclusion          | Arrange access, sound and acoustic screens so six neighbours can participate without disturbing a quiet space.                            |
| 8. Create value people need    | Enterprise, responsible finance | Improve a sample, set a batch and price; serve four customers, cover trial costs and limit waste. Fictional tokens are not income claims. |
| 9. Connect the city            | Transport, coordination         | Set crossing phases, route and dispatch spacing; deliver four loads without crossing conflicts or queues.                                 |
| 10. Build trust and teams      | Leadership, cooperation         | Match qualified crews, agree supervised handover order and reserve joint inspection.                                                      |
| 11. Protect tomorrow           | Environment, resilience         | Separate materials, clear drainage and create a rain garden; test storm runoff and compost contamination.                                 |
| 12. Leave something that lasts | Stewardship, city systems       | Complete eleven earlier outcomes and six prototypes; give residents a maintenance team, repair reserve and operating guide.               |

The recurring five-step mission structure is: inspect the need and initial conditions; change the first physical lever; change or deliberately retain the second; change or deliberately retain the third; operate a trial and inspect its consequences. Chapter 1, market repair, clinic work and teaching retain their more specific existing physical sequences. Every trial records its tested fictional outcome. Changing a lever invalidates the result. Failed trials explain the causal problem; passing outcomes expose a reflection and a clear next chapter. Chapters can be explored through the worksite selector; exploration alone does not earn completion, and handover checks every required outcome.

## Growth without repetitive construction

Six reusable prototypes remain separate worksites. A successful construction enables its family of crew-built models. Outcome-count targets grow from a small batch to seventy reserved parcels by the finale, with no duplicated parcel IDs, unlimited tower generation or airport replication. The final handover requires all six prototypes to be tested; most chapter challenges are about useful services and people. The visible city is fictional and does not assert verified real-world impact or operating capacity.

Activity yards use the relevant existing district. Selecting a system chapter temporarily hides that construction prototype; selecting construction restores its actual saved-in-session stage. Other campaign geometry and campaign state are restored when leaving the preview. Cached construction recipes and simple bounded chapter geometry avoid a separate 3D engine or new external assets.

## Guidance and orientation

The current construction instruction is large and bold. Its action button appears before progress counters and optional controls. Instructions are not an answer key for the new system trials: players see initial conditions, adjust city objects and test consequences.

On a portrait touch device, a real Start or Continue gesture attempts fullscreen and landscape orientation where supported. A visible Allow rotation button releases orientation and respects that choice for the rest of the session. Browsers that reject or lack locking show a dismissible sideways-play reminder. Playing in portrait always remains possible. Browser capability mocks verify success, failure and recovery; physical-phone testing remains necessary.

The orientation behavior follows the platform APIs documented by MDN: https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/ScreenOrientation/lock and https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/Element/requestFullscreen.

## Expansion rule

Developers add a chapter's independent domain rules, physical objects, observable result, recovery feedback, alternative strategies where feasible, reflection and actual-controller test. Do not equate a new label or a repeated button sequence with a new useful outcome. Player testing must establish difficulty and enjoyment before claiming the game is perfected or replacing production.
