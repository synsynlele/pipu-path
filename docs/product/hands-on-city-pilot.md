# Hands-on city pilot — superseded

The initial drainage tile-board experiment was rejected during user review. It is replaced by the character-driven market street pilot described in [market-street-hands-on.md](market-street-hands-on.md).

The new direction keeps the player inside the existing city: walk to objects, carry materials, repair structures, push obstacles, protect stock and test whether the market works. The earlier separate board is no longer a playable mode.
