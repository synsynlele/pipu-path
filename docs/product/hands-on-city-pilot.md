# Hands-on city pilot

The playing field is the 3D scene. A player changes the drainage layout and tests its consequences rather than selecting an answer and watching an unrelated build.

## Playable slice

From the entrance choose **Build directly in the city**. Three homes need drainage. Tap a tool, then tap ground to place drains or basins, remove construction, or clear brown blockages. A blue outlet accepts runoff. A connected basin increases capacity; disconnected storage does nothing. All three homes must connect to the outlet, within fifteen materials. Players can route around blockages and use different branching layouts.

**Test with rain** displays rainfall and marks flooded homes blue and protected homes green. Feedback identifies missing connections or capacity. Undo and reset allow another attempt. Success is shown only when the model protects all three homes. No real-world XP, campaign credit or evidence is generated. A Real Quest link provides a next action.

The initial layout is retained only in the current page session, including exit and re-entry. Existing optional campaign saves are untouched. The older campaign remains available from the entrance during pilot evaluation.

## Expansion contract

Reuse tool selection, spatial hit testing, materials, immutable edits, test feedback, undo and accessible controls. Each future sector needs its own causal model and visible consequences: food delivery must not become drainage with renamed tiles. Transport, power and agriculture follow only after players demonstrate unassisted comprehension and willingness to improve a failed design.

## Acceptance and limits

Automated checks cover model constraints and real-controller entrance, tile selection by keyboard and projected raycast, failure, success, undo, campaign isolation and exit focus. Physical-device layout, visual clarity, touch accuracy and player enjoyment require hands-on acceptance. The managed browser preview launcher currently passes unsupported flags to Next.js; no browser screenshot or GPU acceptance is claimed. Branch deployments are disabled to avoid Vercel quota usage. This pilot is not a claim that the twelve-chapter conversion is complete.
