# Build and stress-test worlds

Authority: user feedback on 27 October 2026 explicitly requests intellectually
stronger challenges, meaningful construction and an interactive cinematic world
across all games. This replaces the default practice presentation, preserving the
Duolingo-style path and earlier story modes/saves. It does not replace real Quests.

## Meaningful projects

- Community: connected essential services and backup energy.
- City: a flood-ready district with shelter, access and protective infrastructure.
- Garden: food production, irrigation, habitat and drought resilience.
- River: treatment on the riverbank, wetlands, maintenance access and flood safety.
- Learning: distributed seat capacity, access, shade and energy resilience.
- Makers: useful production, energy demand and waste processing.
- Food: cold storage, last-mile access, energy and heat resilience.

Every world is a 12-by-8 editable system with reserved HQ/residential zones,
construction budgets, a connected service-corridor network, facility activation,
energy supply/demand, a three-square service radius, capacity and ecological goals.
River terrain, treatment placement, irrigation, recycling and cooling introduce
world-specific rules. There is no single exact blueprint: tests verify alternate
working designs. These are simplified fictional units, not engineering predictions
or health, agricultural, food-safety or business advice.

Run normal operation before facing the disclosed disruption. Repair the same
project and pass its stress test to complete a practice mission. Missions increase
service demand and environmental requirements; the board carries forward. The
19-node path now describes these evaluated build missions: three per world except
the city's one combined project. Existing story saves are not translated into
claims that the new simulations were completed. Story practice remains available
inside each world, and real XP/Quest progress is unchanged.

## Interaction and presentation

Players drag modules onto the scene, reposition/swap existing modules, clear with
full refunds, pan the camera, zoom, or use equivalent tool-plus-square keyboard/tap
placement. The world renders original SVG facilities, crops, treatment tanks,
solar panels, buildings and vegetation. Connected network traffic and residents
animate; flood, heat, drought and outage visuals communicate changed conditions.
Pause cancels a pending test without recording completion. Reduced motion removes
traffic/rain/resident animation. This is a 2D systems-building game, not a 3D engine,
real-time physics simulation or proof of entertainment/learning effectiveness.

## State, privacy and ownership

Optional browser saves use `pipupath.simulation.v1:<user>:<world>` and contain only
bounded tile enums and evaluated before/after blueprint proofs. Parse re-evaluates
all saved completions. Invalid/cross-world/oversized records are rejected. Saving
is off until explicitly enabled or a compatible save is resumed. Cloud saves are
not included. Clearing/disabled browser storage cannot block play. No learner
narrative, credential, evidence, verified XP or capability claim is stored here.

Content design owns world briefs and balance. Engineering owns the simulation
rules/controls. QA owns solvability, failures, accessibility, save recovery and
phone testing. Founder approval is not required for every blueprint or learner
attempt. Reusable modules and versioned specifications allow many projects to
benefit from each control/rule improvement; do not mass-produce levels before
observing real players attempt them.

## Verification boundaries

Automated fixtures exercise every mission under normal/disrupted conditions,
alternate designs, disconnected/unpowered facilities, river placement, waste,
drought, flood, heat, terrain/budget guards and validated save proofs. UI tests
cover normal-before-stress gating, pause, save/resume, keyboard placement and failed
attempt diagnostics. Exact deployed UI and physical Android proof are separate.
