# ADR 0002: No simulated product state

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-07-24

## Decision

Unimplemented capability surfaces must state that they are unavailable or be
absent. They may not display sample profiles, progress, recommendations,
projects, networks, or AI results as though those were real.

## Consequence

Stage 1 demonstrates only the platform foundation. Product behavior begins with
real persistence and authorization in Stage 2.
