# PipuPath Engineering Constitution

**Status:** Locked  
**Authority:** Primary engineering authority  
**Effective:** 2026-07-24

## Mission

PipuPath must move a person through one coherent loop:

1. Join.
2. Discover early potential signals.
3. Select a Journey.
4. Develop through HQLS Quests.
5. Create evidence.
6. Evolve the Human Potential Profile.
7. Build a Project.
8. Find relevant Builders.
9. Collaborate.
10. Deploy capability.
11. Record outcomes.
12. Create credible impact.
13. Receive a larger developmental opportunity.

PipuPath is complete only when development inside the platform becomes useful
outside the platform.

## Governing principles

1. **One operating system.** Capabilities must advance the same developmental
   loop, not become disconnected feature islands.
2. **Truthful state.** Never present fabricated data, fake persistence,
   simulated intelligence, or a completion state that the system cannot prove.
3. **Persistent by design.** Important user state must have an explicit owner,
   lifecycle, durable representation, and recovery strategy.
4. **Explainable decisions.** Recommendations and profile changes must retain
   their evidence, rules, model/version provenance, and user-visible rationale.
5. **Secure by default.** Authorization is enforced at data and action
   boundaries. UI visibility is not authorization.
6. **Privacy by necessity.** Collect the minimum developmental data required,
   classify sensitive data, define retention, and protect young users.
7. **Accessible and inclusive.** Core journeys must work with keyboard,
   assistive technology, reduced motion, narrow screens, and constrained
   connectivity.
8. **Testable slices.** A capability is incomplete without deterministic tests
   at the lowest useful level and an end-to-end proof of its critical path.
9. **Observable operation.** Failures and important transitions must produce
   structured, privacy-safe telemetry.
10. **Stage discipline.** Implement only the authorized stage. Record completion
    evidence and stop at its boundary.
11. **Replaceable infrastructure.** Domain rules must not depend directly on
    vendors, frameworks, transports, or model providers.
12. **Human agency.** AI may clarify, synthesize, or recommend; it must not
    silently define a person, fabricate evidence, or conceal uncertainty.

## Definition of done

A vertical slice is done only when its behavior, persistence, authorization,
validation, error states, accessibility, logging, and tests are complete and
its implementation record is current.

## Final rule

Build PipuPath as one coherent human-development operating system, not as a
collection of attractive screens or disconnected features. Every capability
must be persistent, explainable, secure, testable, and connected to the user’s
movement from discovery to development, deployment, contribution, and impact.
