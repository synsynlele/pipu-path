## One experiment per participant

Ask each participant to choose a useful real-life problem, complete one practical
Quest, bring back honest proof and reflect on what happened. No reward for merely
reading or opening the app. No guaranteed income or capability claim.

Collect just three answers:

1. Where did you get stuck?
2. What did you actually do or create outside the app?
3. What one change would make the next Quest easier?

Use an anonymous participant code, not a learner name/email/private reflection.
Do not upload identity documents, credentials or safeguarding details into the
feedback register. Safety/privacy reports go to the monitored owner contact.
The CSV template is a manual register, not an in-app feedback feature.
